Technician is deployed with one worker per origin:

{{- range $origin := .Values.origins }}
  - {{ include "technician.fullname" $ }}-{{ $origin.id }}  (ORIGIN_ID={{ $origin.id }})
{{- end }}

Each origin runs a single replica and exposes /metrics on port {{ .Values.service.port }}.

Scrape it:
{{- if .Values.podMonitor.enabled }}
  A PodMonitor was created. Ensure its labels match your Prometheus
  podMonitorSelector. Metrics carry an `origin` label per worker.
{{- else }}
  PodMonitor is disabled. Point Prometheus at the per-origin Services, e.g.
    kubectl get svc -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}
{{- end }}

Scaling note:
  Add capacity by adding entries to `.Values.origins` (new vantage points).
  Do NOT raise replicas within an origin — identical replicas collide on
  {check,origin} metric series and multiply load on your targets.
  Sharding one origin's checks across workers needs probe filtering:
  https://github.com/jesseheady/technician/issues/15
